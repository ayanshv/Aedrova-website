A familiar start, wherever you work.

  1. Choose the verified installer for your computer when available.

  2. On Mac, open the disk image and drag Aedrova into Applications. On Windows, run the installer and follow its setup steps.

  3. Open Aedrova and sign in with the same Google account you use here.

  4. Follow the short setup to choose your appearance, name your agent and connect or start a project. GitHub setup is available when you’re ready.

Make yourself at home. The dashboard tour is optional, and you can pause or skip it. Open Help → Getting started or Explore Aedrova whenever you need a refresher.

For future updates, use Help → Check for updates in the app. Quit Aedrova before installing an update. Keep your previous installer until the new version opens successfully; your local project files stay in their original folders.
