1. Information we collect

Website and waitlist. Joining the waitlist collects your email address and consent to receive Aedrova launch updates. Enterprise inquiries collect the contact and project information you submit. Support requests include your contact information and the contents of your request. Registration does not create an account or subscription. Waitlist addresses are not used as AI build context.

Accounts and workspaces. When account access is enabled, Google sign-in through Supabase provides identity and profile information, such as your email, name and avatar. We process workspace membership, roles, profile settings, messages, replies, reactions, uploaded files and other content you or your teammates supply. Workspace owners and authorized members can access information according to their permissions.

Buds and connected services. We process your Bud’s name, appearance, instructions, role, preferences and authorized connections. Connecting a service can provide account identifiers, resource selections, access credentials and content available under the permissions you approve. Examples include repositories, documents, design files, social account information, deployment information and Stripe account/balance information. The actual provider consent screen and selected resources determine the connection’s scope.

Meetings and voice. When available and enabled, meeting and voice features process participant information and audio/video needed to provide the call. Transcription processes submitted audio and resulting text. Meeting summaries and transcripts can contain personal or confidential information. Participants must be informed and any required consent obtained before recording or transcription. A call does not mean that recording, transcription or AI access is automatically enabled.

Operational and billing information. Hosting and service systems process IP addresses, request times, error/security information and service usage. We retain subscription state, payment references, run identifiers and AI usage accounting where those features are enabled. Stripe handles card/payment details; this website does not store full card numbers.

2. Why we use information

We use information to operate the service, authenticate accounts, deliver team collaboration and requested AI work, manage authorized connections, prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, provide support, maintain security, process billing and meet applicable legal obligations. Waitlist emails are used for launch updates and administration of your registration. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. AI, project access and connector permissions

Invoking an AI participant can transmit your prompt, permitted workspace context, relevant files, project content and authorized connector results to the selected provider. Aedrova may use OpenAI or Anthropic, or a coding runtime you connect, depending on the feature and configuration. Only enable a provider or connection if you are authorized to share the relevant data. Do not submit credentials, regulated health information or other highly sensitive information that the service is not configured to handle.

AI processing is not exclusively on your device. Providers’ retention and processing rules depend on their service and account configuration; we do not promise zero retention or that every provider excludes all data from training. Review the selected provider’s applicable terms and privacy information. Aedrova does not use your workspace content to train its own general-purpose AI models.

Automatic planning/execution permissions allow the configured workflow to plan, edit and run supported commands in its build environment. They do not make every connected account universally available to every Bud. Review permissions and generated work. Disconnecting in Aedrova stops future use through that stored connection, but may not revoke permission at the provider; revoke access in the provider’s account settings as well. Data already sent to a provider is subject to that provider’s controls.

4. Who receives information

Information is shared with authorized workspace participants, service providers needed to deliver the requested functionality, providers you connect, and selected AI providers. Current infrastructure includes Supabase for authentication/database/storage, Render for website/service hosting, Cloudflare for domain/network services, Google for sign-in, and Stripe for enabled payment or Stripe connector functions. Optional integrations include GitHub, Figma, Notion, Supabase, Instagram/Meta, TikTok and Vercel; managed search can use Brave. Availability varies and some connections remain under testing.

We may disclose information when legally required, to respond to valid legal process, protect users or investigate fraud/security incidents. If the business is transferred, relevant information may transfer subject to applicable law and notice where required. We do not grant other workspaces access to your content merely because they use Aedrova.

5. Cookies, browser storage and tracking

We use browser storage for appearance preferences and website introduction answers, and session cookies when sign-in is enabled. Infrastructure may use security-related cookies and logs. We do not currently run advertising pixels or cross-site behavioral advertising on the website. Because that tracking is not used, browser Do Not Track signals do not change its behavior. Third-party sites you open, including sign-in and connector pages, have their own practices. Fonts and website background media are served by Aedrova.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Waitlist email addresses are encrypted in our Supabase database and retained for up to one year, unless you request earlier removal. Active workspace content and connector settings are retained while needed to provide the service or until an authorized deletion request is completed. Disconnecting a connector removes its stored grant from active use; it does not delete the source content in the external service.

Session and temporary response data are subject to expiry/maintenance. Billing, usage and security records may be retained when necessary for accounting, dispute handling, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Deleted data may remain in backups until those backups rotate and cannot always be removed immediately from third-party systems. We do not claim one universal retention period for every category or an instant deletion guarantee.

We use access controls, encrypted storage for service credentials and sensitive backend records, and secure transport where supported. Encryption does not mean that no authorized service can decrypt data to perform its function. No service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account and report suspected exposure promptly through support.

12. Practical controls and limits

14. Categories of personal information

The following categories describe information Aedrova can process when the corresponding feature is used. They are not a statement that every category is collected from every visitor.

  • Identifiers and account details: email address, display name, username, avatar, authentication identifiers, workspace/member identifiers and network address.
  • Profile and team information: optional biography, role/title, presence, custom status, membership, access role, channel participation and preferences you supply.
  • User content and communications: messages, replies, reactions, attachments, code, documents, support correspondence, prompts, voice recordings, meeting text and generated results.
  • Connection information: provider and account identifiers, resource references, authorization grants and tokens, granted scopes and retrieved information needed for an authorized task.
  • Commercial and service records: plan, subscription/customer references, billing state, payment events, request usage, task accounting and relevant purchase/support records.
  • Technical information: browser/device or request information exposed during operation, session/OAuth state, timestamps, failure information and security/rate-limit events.
  • Preferences and derived task information: Bud instructions, chosen work style, selected context, task state and summaries or other outputs produced from supplied information.

Some content may contain sensitive information even though we do not ask for it: health details, government identifiers, confidential personnel information, precise locations or financial records, for example. Access credentials are sensitive connection information. Do not upload highly sensitive or regulated content unless an appropriate written arrangement and technical configuration support that use. Ordinary account authentication or an Enterprise inquiry alone does not establish such an arrangement.

Aedrova does not intentionally create voiceprints, face-recognition templates or biometric identity profiles from call media. Processing a voice recording to deliver audio or text is different from identifying someone by a biometric template. Audio, avatars and transcripts can nevertheless identify a person and should be shared with care.

18. How retention is determined

Retention depends on the data category, whether the feature is active, your organization’s instructions, the reason for processing and applicable obligations. We consider whether a record remains necessary to deliver requested work, whether it is needed to resolve an incident or payment dispute, and whether a lawful deletion or preservation request applies. We do not retain data indefinitely merely because storage is available.

  • Waitlist: registrations are configured with a one-year expiry; verified earlier removal is available through support. Expiry and maintenance govern active registration records, while backups have separate handling.
  • Sessions and temporary state: authentication, OAuth and other temporary records have their configured expirations. Browser state and an expired server session may have different lifetimes.
  • Managed inference response cache: backend maintenance clears cached response content after its 24-hour window. Usage/monetary accounting can remain. This rule is not a 24-hour deletion promise for chat history, all provider records or saved build results.
  • Saved meeting text: the supported shared-text window is currently 30 days where enabled, subject to earlier withdrawal/deletion controls and actual maintenance. Provider-held audio/text and backup copies are distinct.
  • Chat, files, voice messages and build records: these can persist for collaboration and history until the relevant supported removal or verified scoped request is completed. Removing a local attachment preview does not necessarily remove its hosted file.
  • Connections: stored grants are used while authorized and are removed from active use when disconnected. Original-service data and other independently granted connections remain separate.
  • Billing, fraud prevention, legal holds and fulfillment records: limited information may be retained to meet legal obligations, document transactions or requests, or handle actual disputes and security incidents.

There is currently no universal automatic account/workspace erasure timer or one-click full export/deletion feature. Support reviews such requests and explains scope and exceptions. We will not describe an administrative access expiry as verified physical deletion from every system. A backup restoration must also account for approved removals; backup retention and provider obligations should be reviewed before relying on a specific deletion deadline.
