Your data. Your choices.

What Aedrova can use, what you can stop, and how to request a copy or removal.

AI contextConnectionsMeetingsData requests

Choose what reaches AI

Messages, uploaded files, meeting text and connector results are different sources of context. A workspace membership does not make every private channel accessible. Buds use the resources granted to them. Coding requests can include accessible workspace history and project context, so review the provider and scope before invoking the agent.

Use available context controls to review or exclude eligible sources. Changing future access cannot recall a prompt or result already sent to an AI provider. Automatic planning/execution controls allow supported work in the build copy; original-project changes and publishing retain their approval gates. Turning that setting off stops new automatic authorization, not an action already completed.

Disconnect a tool

  1. Open your Bud’s connectors and select the connection you want to manage.
  2. Disconnect the stored connection to stop its future use through Aedrova.
  3. Visit the provider’s authorized-app settings and revoke Aedrova there as well.
  4. Check any separately granted accounts or resources. Removing one grant may not remove another.

Disconnect does not delete the repository, page, design file, payment records or social account in the original service. The provider manages data already stored in its own systems. Do not submit access tokens in a support message.

Meeting text needs separate consent

Where enabled, meeting privacy settings separately cover saving text and reusing it as AI context. All present participants must consent under the supported workflow. A microphone permission alone is not consent to shared transcription.

Meeting privacy offers withdrawal of AI access and deletion of saved shared meeting text. Withdrawal affects future use of that meeting’s shared text. Deletion is consequential for all participants, so read the confirmation. An end-of-meeting review can correct the transcription before confirmation; that confirmation does not override a participant’s missing or withdrawn consent. Hosted transcription still depends on available infrastructure.

Chat, files and local projects

Use message removal for messages you are authorized to remove. Removing a message is not a promise to erase every database record, prior notification, recipient copy, downloaded attachment or backup. Channel permissions continue to govern private files. A voice recording you send is a channel attachment, not a disappearing meeting chunk.

Your original local project and provider-created copies are separate from hosted account data. Downloaded files, local build copies and exports can remain on a computer after logout or a hosted deletion request. Remove local copies you no longer need using the relevant app or operating system; keep work you are legally required to retain. Do not delete an original project simply to disconnect Aedrova.

Request access, export, correction or deletion

Email contact@aedrova.com from your account or waitlist address. State the request type and the relevant workspace name. Specify whether you mean personal account information, a particular file/meeting, or an entire workspace. A whole-workspace request requires authority; one member cannot request other members’ unrelated data.

We verify identity and scope, explain retained records or limitations, and respond according to applicable law. Account/workspace export and permanent deletion currently use this support process; there is currently no one-click automated erase/export feature. Do not email government ID, passwords or keys. Deleting data is separate from cancelling a subscription. Backups and third-party systems can have different removal schedules.

Cancel future renewals

Where paid subscriptions are enabled, the workspace owner can use Manage team billing or email contact@aedrova.com from the billing account. Cancellation stops renewal; access normally continues through the paid period. Uninstalling or deleting data does not cancel billing.

Request a refund

Email contact@aedrova.com with your account address, workspace name, receipt reference, payment date and a short explanation. Do not send card numbers or bank information. We verify the transaction and review duplicate or unauthorized charges, delivery problems and service defects.

How eligibility works

Unless the purchase specifies a more favorable policy or applicable law requires otherwise, fees are not automatically prorated or refunded for unused time. Statutory cancellation rights, remedies for defective service and other mandatory consumer rights remain intact. We explain the decision and any applicable remedy; we do not promise approval of every request or a fixed bank-processing time.

What you pay

The checkout must show the workspace price, renewal interval and applicable taxes before confirmation. Eligible teammates do not incur separate member charges. Aedrova does not automatically bill AI overages; separate third-party provider charges can apply when you authorize that provider.

Cookies and browser storage.

Aedrova uses functional storage to remember your choices and provide enabled account features. The current website does not run advertising pixels or cross-site behavioral advertising.

What is stored

  • Appearance: your light, dark or system preference.
  • Introduction choices: website onboarding answers saved in your browser.
  • Account sessions: secure session and OAuth state cookies when sign-in is enabled.
  • Infrastructure: security or request information handled by the hosting/network services.

Your controls

Use to allow persistent preferences or keep essential storage only. Both choices are equally available. Until you allow preferences, new introduction answers and appearance changes are held only in the current page memory. This choice does not authorize advertising or analytics.

Use the theme switch to change appearance. Browser settings let you inspect or clear site data and restrict cookies. Clearing data can sign you out or reset preferences; it does not delete hosted workspace content or cancel a subscription. Essential sign-in storage is needed for enabled account features to work.

There are currently no advertising or analytics categories to enable. The storage-choice record is kept until you change it or clear browser storage; saved preferences remain until you remove them. Essential OAuth/session cookies follow the account session lifetime. Browser Do Not Track signals do not introduce additional collection. If tracking changes, we will update the notices and implement consent or opt-out controls as required before enabling it. Provider authorization pages have separate policies.

Build responsibly.

This policy forms part of the Terms of service. It applies to humans, agent instructions, uploaded content and connected-tool actions.

Use only what you are authorized to use

Do not access another workspace’s content, steal credentials, evade channel permissions, collect data without a lawful basis or grant a Bud access to an account you do not control. Obtain required permissions for files, intellectual property and meeting recording/transcription. A Bud does not create rights you otherwise lack.

Keep people and systems safe

Do not use Aedrova for malware, exploitation, fraud, illegal surveillance, abuse, threats or harassment, child exploitation, or instructions that facilitate unlawful harm. Do not upload prohibited material or use connected social accounts for spam, impersonation or deceptive campaigns. Respect providers’ own terms.

Respect service limits

Do not bypass plan capacity, server rate limits, security checks or payment restrictions. Do not deliberately overload the service, flood transcription/upload queues, scrape private content or conceal unsafe tool actions inside documents or prompts. Review generated code and external actions before approving them.

Reporting and enforcement

We may restrict abusive activity, investigate credible reports and preserve information required for security or law. Contact support to question an access restriction. For a vulnerability, follow security reporting guidance; do not obtain additional private data to prove it. We do not currently operate a paid bug bounty or promise a reward.

Security & trust.

Aedrova uses authenticated workspace access, channel permissions, scoped connections and backend checks. Release testing and independent review remain part of the launch process.

What the controls mean

Private content is governed by membership and authorization. Upload checks validate content before publication. Provider credentials are kept out of frontend assets and protected in the supported credential storage. Transport encryption does not make the product end-to-end encrypted: authorized backend or AI services may process readable content for the requested feature.

We do not claim SOC 2 certification, a guaranteed absence of vulnerabilities, a universal residency commitment or a public uptime SLA. The current Beta is still under validation. Limits, outage recovery and cross-workspace testing remain release gates.

Report a vulnerability

Email contact@aedrova.com with “Security report” in the subject. Include the affected feature, minimal reproduction steps and expected versus actual access, using your own test accounts. Redact credentials and private content. Do not access more data, disrupt availability, socially engineer users or publish secrets.

We will coordinate a safe investigation. There is no paid bounty, blanket authorization to test third-party infrastructure or automatic legal safe-harbor promise on this page. Ask before testing beyond normal use of your own accounts.
