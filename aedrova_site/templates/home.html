A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Let’s clear a little space.

Does my agent read every conversation?

When you ask it to build, it searches across the workspace context you’re authorized to access, rather than relying only on your latest prompt. That includes accessible conversations, supported small text attachments and confirmed decisions. It stays quiet until invoked, and workspace and channel permissions still apply. Consented video-call transcripts and connected design tools are planned additions.

Will it change my local project automatically?

With your saved execution permission, the agent plans, codes and tests in a separate local copy. You review before applying changes to your original project. GitHub publication requires a separate approval.

Are Codex and Claude Code included?

The paid beta is being prepared with an included AI allowance through Aedrova’s managed provider access. Weekly includes $1 and monthly includes $10 of shared workspace AI usage, with one active build and no automatic overage charges. The current internal alpha uses separate provider setup; checkout stays closed until the paid experience is ready.

Can we use it for meetings?

Meeting calls, screen sharing and consented transcription are next on the roadmap. They are not available in the current beta.

What platform is Aedrova built for?

Aedrova is a Mac desktop app, downloaded from this website. The public installer will be available after signing and release verification.
