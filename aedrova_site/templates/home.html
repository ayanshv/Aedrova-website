A few things to know

What can my agent see?

Permitted conversations, shared files and selected connected resources. Private-source access is checked for each request.

Who approves changes?

You review before changes reach your original files. Publishing requires a separate approval.

How do meeting notes work?

Audio transcription requires current consent from every participant. AI reuse is a separate choice. You review notes before sharing them as context. Screen sharing does not give the agent automatic visual understanding of your screen.

When can I get it?

Mac and Windows releases are being prepared. Public rollout pending final acceptance. Join the waitlist for availability.
