Aedrova FAQ

By Aedrova team · Published October 22, 2026

What does Aedrova do?

Aedrova brings team conversations, configurable AI teammates called Buds, and supported coding-agent workflows into a shared workspace. Read the product guide.

Who is it for?

Founders, small software teams and independent builders coordinating product decisions and implementation.

Can I use or download Aedrova now?

{% if waitlist_only %}Public downloads and checkout are not open. The waitlist collects launch interest; joining does not activate software or AI access.{% else %}Access depends on current invitations, enabled services and release readiness. Check downloads and Beta availability.{% endif %}

Does a plan cover my teammates?

The intended paid model is per workspace, with no separate member charge. The plans page lists current prices, offer dates and capacity descriptions. The shared early-access deadline does not restart for each signup.

What are the Bud roles?

Builder, Designer, Marketing, Finance, Research and Product. You can configure their names, appearance and working preferences. Their actual capabilities depend on supported features and permissions.

Can every connector be used by everyone?

No. Provider approval, account type, selected resources, scopes and actual integration readiness determine availability. A listed integration is not proof of public provider approval. See connector troubleshooting.

Do Buds have unrestricted account access?

No. Access is limited by the supported authorization and the requesting user's permissions. Private channels and workspaces retain their access controls. Read Data & AI controls.

Do AI changes publish automatically?

Supported agents can plan and work in a separate build copy under the selected configuration. Applying changes to the original project and publishing retain their approval requirements. Review and test generated work.

Are meetings always transcribed?

No. Hosted transcription depends on infrastructure, configuration, permissions and acceptance checks. Do not assume every meeting is recorded or every transcript is shared with AI.

Can Marketing Bud generate images or video?

That capability is planned and is not presented as a released customer feature.

Is all processing local?

No. Enabled hosted features and providers may process authorized prompts, files or audio. See Privacy and service providers.

How can I get help or request deletion?

Contact contact@aedrova.com, visit Support, or follow the data-request process.
