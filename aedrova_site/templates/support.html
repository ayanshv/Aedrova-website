Email is the best way to share a detailed request. Phone support is subject to availability; we do not promise 24/7 coverage or a fixed response time.

Help us find the problem

Include your account email, operating system, app version and a short description of what happened. If helpful, attach a screenshot with private content removed. Never send passwords, API keys, connector tokens, full card numbers or government identity documents.

Accounts and connectors

Use the same Google account on the website and desktop app. If a connector stops working, check its status and authorized resource selection, then reconnect if needed. Disconnecting Aedrova does not necessarily revoke permission at the provider; revoke it in that provider’s settings too. Contact us if the account or workspace shown is incorrect.

Privacy and deletion

Follow our data request instructions for a copy, correction or removal. Our provider inventory explains where authorized processing can occur.

For requests to remove your waitlist registration, email us from the address you registered. For access, correction, export or account/workspace deletion, identify the relevant account and your request. We may verify identity and workspace authority. See our Privacy policy for processing, retention and your choices.

Security reports

Read our security reporting guidance before testing or sharing evidence.

Email support with “Security report” in the subject and a minimal description. Do not include stolen credentials or other users’ data, and do not access additional data to prove an issue. If your account appears compromised, revoke relevant provider permissions and secure your sign-in account.

Availability and updates

Some Beta features and external connectors remain under testing. A provider outage or an ineligible provider account can interrupt a task. Contact support for current service information; this page is not a live status monitor.
